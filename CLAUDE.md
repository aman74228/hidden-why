# Hidden Why — Studio Rules (read every session)
Channel: Hidden Why — ancient engineering and everyday mysteries, explained in a minute.
ONE reusable studio. Never create a new project per Short. Each Short = a brief in /briefs + its voiceover in /audio.

## Stack
Remotion + three.js (React Three Fiber), 1080x1920, 30 fps. No AI video generators.

## Cast (reusable rigs; reskin outfits per story, keep faces/colours)
- Chotu: asks the question in the hook. Small kid, oversized round head, orange kurta, cowlick. Wide eyes, points.
- Vik: the mocker, guesses wrong 3 times, proven wrong at payoff. Tall, lanky, teal jacket, smug half-lidded eyes, raised eyebrow. Arms crossed, smirk.
- Dada: old craftsman, never explains, only shows. Short, round, white beard, cream shawl, small round spectacles. Calm nod, tiny smile.
Poses: idle, talk, point, laugh, cross-arms, shock, nod.

## Look
- Flat 2D characters (round heads, dot eyes, bean bodies, stick limbs, 4px dark outline) inside lit 3D toon sets.
- Palette: warm saffron-gold key light, deep indigo-teal shadows, never pure black. Accent #F2A33A.
- Camera low, wide, always gliding; a cut or strong camera move every 2–3 s, 3+ object inserts per Short. Helpers: dolly, orbit, push-in.
- Sets: CC0 textures + HDRI (scripts/fetch_assets.py), 8+ props per set, light beams, bloom, particles, subtle grain. Look playbook: .claude/skills/cartoon-3d-short.
- Captions: bold white, dark outline, sentence case, 1–2 words at a time, ~68 px, centred at 70% height, synced to the voiceover.
- Title cards: indigo panel, #F2A33A border, small upper label + big lower word.
- Ending: hold payoff 0.5 s, hard cut so it loops into the first frame.

## Audio
- Voiceover is generated by Claude Code via the ElevenLabs API using voice.json (eleven_v3 + Liam); the brief's script is the source, with v3 audio tags ([curious], [sarcastic], [warm], [deadpan]) and "..." for pauses. Takes go to audio/takes/ (`scripts/voiceover.py <slug> <take>`, max 3 per Short); `scripts/tighten_vo.py` trims silent gaps and finalises audio/<slug>.mp3 + captions.
- Word timestamps come from the ElevenLabs with-timestamps response (scripts/voiceover.py). Fallback: align the MP3 to the brief's script (faster-whisper; if its model download is blocked, split words across speech segments found with ffmpeg silencedetect).

### Sound sourcing (Freesound)
- Freesound API (key in FREESOUND_API_KEY). Search https://freesound.org/apiv2/search/text/ with token auth.
- Filter: license:"Creative Commons 0", duration fitting the cue, avg_rating:[3.5 TO 5]. Sort by rating/downloads.
- Download the "preview-hq-mp3" of the best match into public/sfx/<name>.mp3. For each cue, audition the top 3 (duration, loudness) and pick the cleanest (no background noise or music).
- Log every file in public/sfx/credits.json: name, freesound id, URL, author, license. CC0 only, never CC-BY or NC.
- NO whoosh/swoosh sounds, ever.
- Library (reusable for all Shorts): rain-soft (light rain ambience, loopable) · coin-scrape · crickets · anvil · metal-ting · pop-soft · drip · shimmer (soft magic sparkle) · gate-creak · ding (cartoon) · footsteps-stone · crowd-murmur-soft.
- Music: Freesound CC0 playful pizzicato/quirky loop, 30 s or more. If no good match, ask the user to supply one.

### Mix (strict) — `scripts/mix.py <slug> <out.wav>` builds it and writes a .levels.json LUFS report
- Voice -14 LUFS.
- Music the whole time, 8–10 dB under the voice, ducked a further 3 dB under speech.
- Every SFX at least 6 dB under the voice.
- Rain at -32 LUFS, fading out at the first cut.
- Nothing peaks above the voice.

4. Render to /out/<slug>.mp4, commit and push.
