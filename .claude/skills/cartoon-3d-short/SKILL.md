# Cartoon 3D Short — Hidden Why edition

Upstream: mromidev/claude-animated-shorts (MIT, see LICENSE). We keep its look
targets and techniques, but **our cast, stories, audio rules and studio code**.
The upstream private reference frames/sheets of another channel are deliberately
NOT included and must never be added to this repo.

## What wins when rules conflict
1. `CLAUDE.md` (cast, palette, captions ON, Freesound CC0 audio, strict mix, workflow).
2. The user's latest message.
3. This skill.
Upstream rules we do NOT use: "no captions", "music +3 dB", synthesized score,
2K export, film grain off. Ours: captions on, CLAUDE.md mix, 1080x1920, subtle grain on.

## Studio map (this repo, not upstream's `src/videos/bridge/`)
- Shot list JSON: `src/shots/<slug>.json` (generated by `scripts/shots_<slug>.py`).
- Sets: `src/sets/` — `ToonSet.tsx` (dispatch, lights, sky), `world.tsx` (textured sets + props),
  `story.tsx` (story hero props, macro inserts), `fx.tsx` (rain + splashes, dust, sparks, steam, light beams),
  `materials.tsx` (CC0 textures from `public/tex/`, HDRI from `public/hdri/`).
- Cast: `src/characters/` (billboard rigs, poses, costumes/props, blinks, breathing).
- Post: `src/scene/ShotScene.tsx` (DOF → bloom → vignette → grain).
- Captions: `src/overlays/Captions.tsx` (sentence case, centred at 70% height).
- Audio: `scripts/voiceover.py` (ElevenLabs, with timestamps) → `scripts/tighten_vo.py` →
  `scripts/mix.py` (strict CLAUDE.md levels, reports LUFS per stem).
- Assets: `scripts/fetch_assets.py` (Poly Haven CC0, logged in `public/tex/credits.json`).

## Look targets (from upstream measurements, `references/reports/`)
- Colour: median luma ~.5, no crushed blacks (our darkest = indigo, never #000), saturated lit chroma.
- Warm key + cool (indigo-teal) shade; light pools/beams also tint characters.
- Depth: DOF on close-ups and inserts only; wides stay sharp. Fog = horizon colour.
- Bloom only on HDR emissives (lamps, forge, sun, glints): threshold ~0.85.
- Camera: every hold creeps (push-in 0.15–0.35 %/f); glides are smootherstep 18–40 f; a cut or strong
  move every 2–3 s; 3+ object inserts per Short; low camera heights; 4–5° rolls on drama.
- Characters: lit by the scene, contact shadow, blink every 1.5–4 s (4 f), visible breathing,
  costume/prop per story (umbrella, satchel, stick, apron), reaction lands 0–3 f before its word.

## Pipeline per Short
1. Brief + tagged script (eleven_v3 tags) → `voiceover.py <slug> <take>` (≤3 takes) → `tighten_vo.py`.
2. `scripts/shots_<slug>.py` cuts on the word timestamps → `src/shots/<slug>.json`.
3. Stills: `bash scripts/stills.sh <slug>` (one per beat) → show the user.
4. Preview: `bash scripts/preview.sh <slug> 10` (first 10 s with the mix).
5. Only after approval: `bash scripts/render.sh <slug>`.

## Readability traps (keep checking)
- Props closer than ~2 m to the lens turn into blobs at the frame edge.
- A foreground prop must be recognisable or gone.
- Signs lit from the side go grey: unlit material for sign faces.
- Flat holds read as freezes: every hold needs a creeping push-in.
- Render temp: Remotion bundles copy `public/` each time; clean `/tmp/remotion-*` between big passes.
- Remotion writes a silent audio track: mux with `-map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0`.

`templates/` holds upstream's bridge source (ink pass, lens blur, grade, painted textures,
audio DSP) as reference for techniques; port pieces into `src/` rather than copying wholesale.
